What We Collect

When you submit a quote request through any of our websites, we collect the information you provide directly, including your name, business name, email address, phone number, business address, and business operations information needed to obtain insurance quotes on your behalf.

When you visit our websites, we collect standard server log data including IP addresses and browser information through our hosting provider. We also use the Meta Pixel on our segment websites to measure ad performance and to show relevant advertising to people who have visited our sites.

When you communicate with us by email, we retain those communications. When you sign a bind confirmation document, we collect your electronic signature and the associated document data through our e-signature provider.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to obtain and present commercial insurance quotes, bind coverage on your behalf, service your policy, and communicate with you about your coverage. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information for any purpose unrelated to your insurance needs.

Who We Share Your Information With

To obtain quotes and bind coverage on your behalf, we share your business information with insurance carriers and wholesalers. We share only what is necessary for underwriting and binding purposes.

We also work with the following service providers who process data on our behalf in connection with operating our platform:

  • Render — application hosting and infrastructure
  • Google Workspace — business email and communications
  • BoldSign — electronic signatures for bind documents
  • Cloudflare R2 — secure document storage for applications, quotes, and policy documents
  • Supabase — authentication and session management for the CID Connect app
  • Meta — advertising analytics via Meta Pixel on our websites
  • Instantly — outbound email campaign delivery

These providers are used solely to operate our business and are not permitted to use your information for their own purposes.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill quoting, binding, and policy servicing obligations, and as required by applicable law and insurance regulations.

Cookies and Tracking

Our segment websites use the Meta Pixel, a tracking technology that helps us measure the effectiveness of our advertising and show our ads to people who have visited our sites. You can adjust how Meta uses data for ads through Meta ad preferences or your Meta account settings.

Your Rights

You may contact us at g@commercialinsurance-direct.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to our legal and regulatory retention obligations.

Colorado residents may have additional rights regarding their personal information under applicable state law. Contact us at g@commercialinsurance-direct.com to make a request.

Children

Our services are intended for business owners and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

US Audience

Our services are directed at businesses operating in the United States. We do not knowingly collect information from individuals outside the United States.
